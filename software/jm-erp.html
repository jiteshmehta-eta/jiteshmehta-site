JM ERP for Supply Chain and Maintenance

Developed by Jitesh Mehta

One system for requisition to payment and for maintaining the plant, built from a working supply chain procedure. It runs on Windows from a single file.

By Jitesh Mehta, Independent Advisor · Version 1.0, November 2026

Download for WindowsZip file, 48 MB. Free. No sign up. Nothing else to install.

What it is

JM ERP is an ERP for the supply chain and maintenance functions of an asset heavy company. It was built by turning a complete supply chain operating procedure into software, chapter by chapter, so the rules of the procedure are enforced by the system and not left to training.

A requisition cannot skip its approval. An order cannot be placed without three quotations or an approved waiver. An invoice is not paid unless the order, the receipt and the invoice agree.

The home screen. Each person sees the tiles for their own job.

Who it is for

  • Supply chain, procurement and warehouse teams who want the procedure and the system to be the same thing.
  • Maintenance and reliability teams who need the asset register connected to the spare parts actually in stock.
  • Finance teams who want every receipt, issue, invoice and payment in the ledger without re-keying.
  • People who already know SAP. The vocabulary and the transaction codes are the ones they use every day, so they can work without training.
  • People who have never used an ERP. The user manual walks through every task with pictures of the real screens.

What it covers

One database, one set of documents, one audit trail

  • Cataloguing. New item requests with a duplicate check before any code is created. The unit of measure is locked once set.
  • Suppliers. Pre-qualification scoring, audits, suspension and removal, and a two person check on every change of bank details.
  • Bidding. Request for quotation or invitation to bid chosen by value, sealed bids, technical then commercial opening, a bid committee, and bid waivers approved one level above normal authority.
  • Contracts. Contracts, framework and rate agreements, variation orders approved on the cumulative contract value, and work completion certificates.
  • Purchasing. Requisitions with a budget check, purchase and service orders, call off orders from frameworks, amendments and emergency procurement.
  • Warehouse. Goods receipt with inspection, discrepancy reports, first in first out issue, returns, gate passes, stock transfers, stock counts, shelf life and replenishment.
  • Invoices and payment. Three way match, payment blocks with the reason stated, and payment runs that leave out any supplier whose bank details are unverified.
  • General ledger. Every stock and invoice movement posts a balanced journal. The trial balance reconciles inventory to the stock records.
  • Delegation of authority. The approval route is decided by the system from the value, and shown before a document is submitted.

Maintenance connected to the storeroom

The asset register runs from the plant down through system group, system, equipment, assembly and sub-assembly to the individual spare part. Every spare part is an item in the material master, so the hierarchy shows what is in stock, what is on order and how long it takes to arrive. An asset hierarchy that does not know the stock is only a drawing.

The asset hierarchy, with live stock against every spare part

  • Equipment master data with class, duty, ratings, documents and a completeness check, so missing data is visible.
  • Bills of material for equipment and for assemblies, with spare criticality and a stock or non stock decision.
  • FMEA with failure, cause and action codes in the style of ISO 14224. The same codes are used when a repair is closed, which builds the failure history.
  • Work orders with permits, time confirmation and preventive plans by calendar or by running hours.
  • Cost by account. Labour, spare parts, utilities, hire, transport and services are each posted to their own ledger account against the equipment's cost centre.

What a job cost, by ledger account, planned against actual

Built to work with AI agents

The system has a connector that lets an AI assistant such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini operate it from a conversation: find items, raise a requisition, record a receipt, run a report. Every action an agent takes goes through the same rules as a person's.

An agent can prepare and submit. It can never approve, verify bank details or release a payment. Those stay with people, and every attempt is recorded.

How to start

  1. Download the zip file and unzip the whole folder, for example to C:\JM-ETA.
  2. Double click JM-ETA.exe. Your browser opens the sign in page. Windows may show a warning the first time because the program is new; choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Sign in as one of the demonstration users listed on the page. The file READ ME FIRST in the folder gives the password.
  4. Follow the user manual for your first requisition, order, receipt and invoice.

Other computers on the same network open it in a browser. Nothing is installed on them. Your data stays in a folder beside the program, on your own computer.

Questions

Is it really free? Yes. You may use and adapt it within your own organisation. You may not sell it, or include it or any adapted version of it in a paid product or service, without written permission. The words "Developed by Jitesh Mehta" must stay in every copy and every adapted version.

Is it ready to run my company? Treat version 1.0 as a system to evaluate. Try it with the demonstration data, then with your own, before you depend on it. It keeps its data in one folder that you must back up yourself, and it is meant for a trusted office network, not the open internet.

Does it replace SAP? It covers the supply chain, maintenance and the ledger postings that come from them. It does not do payroll, sales, or statutory financial statements.

Can it be adapted to our procedure? Approval limits, thresholds, business units and roles are settings you change yourself. For anything deeper, write to me.

Why does my name appear on every screen? The author's name and notice are part of the program. A copy in which they have been removed or changed does not start.

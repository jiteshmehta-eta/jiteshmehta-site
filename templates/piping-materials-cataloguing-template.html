The Piping Materials Cataloguing Template

A free Excel template that stops duplicate and incomplete material codes at the request, before they ever reach your material master.

By Jitesh Mehta, Independent Advisor · Version 2.3, November 2026

Download the templateExcel workbook, 26 sheets, about 12 MB. Free. No sign up. No macros.

Why this template exists

Most bad material master data starts at the request. A request that leaves out the schedule, the class or the material grade gets coded anyway. An item that already exists gets a second code because nobody searched first. Duplicate codes split your stock, hide what you already own and inflate your inventory.

This template stops both problems at the source. A line cannot reach READY TO SUBMIT until it is complete, follows the standards and has been checked against your material master.

Who it is for

  • Requesters and piping engineers raising new material requests.
  • Warehouse supervisors who certify that an item is genuinely new and not already in stock.
  • Cataloguers and master data teams who create Unique Identification Codes (UIC).
  • Materials, supply chain and maintenance leaders in oil and gas, refining, petrochemicals and EPC who want clean data without buying another system.

The four step process

1. SearchRequester searches the master for an existing UIC
2. RequestOnly if none fits, one line per item
3. CertifyWarehouse supervisor confirms it is new
4. CreateCataloguer creates a UIC only for a truly new item

Every new code passes three people and three checks

  1. Search first. On the UIC Search sheet, the requester searches the material master by keyword or part number. If an existing UIC fits, they use it and stop.
  2. Fill the template. Only when no UIC fits. One line per item, on the commodity sheet that matches it. Each line checks itself against the master as it is typed.
  3. Warehouse certifies. The warehouse supervisor rechecks the specification and certifies that the item is new and that nothing similar is in stock.
  4. Cataloguer creates or rejects. The cataloguer checks the master again and creates a new UIC only for an item never ordered before. Otherwise they assign the existing UIC or reject the line.

What is inside

Nine commodity sheets: Pipe, Non Metallic Pipe, Fittings, Flanges, Gaskets, Bolting, Valves, Tubing (tubing and tube fittings) and Specialties. Each has a worked example in row 6, drop down lists for standard values, and fields marked Mandatory, If applicable, Optional or Auto.

Seventeen supporting sheets: Read Me, UIC Search, Request Header, Approvals, Material Master, Health Check, Attribute Gaps, AI Lookup, AI Findings, Noun Modifier, Equivalents, Abbreviations, UNSPSC Map, Attribute Rules, Attribute Engine, Lists and References.

  • Smart grade lists. On the Pipe sheet, pick the material specification first and the Grade list shows only the grades for that specification.
  • Sour service built in. NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 for production, NACE MR0103/ISO 17945 for refining, and HIC (TM0284) and SSC (TM0177) testing options.
  • Short text and PO text built for you. A 40 character short text from the noun, modifier and key attributes, and a full purchase order text from every filled field.
  • UNSPSC proposed automatically from the official code set, for the cataloguer to confirm.
  • Additive manufacturing ready. An optional section records whether an item could be 3D printed later, using the ISO/ASTM 52900 process categories.

The five level duplicate check

Paste an export of your material master into the Material Master sheet (it holds 20,000 rows and can be extended). Every request line is then checked against it:

  1. Same UIC already exists in the master.
  2. Same Manufacturer Part Number, ignoring spaces, hyphens, dots and other punctuation.
  3. Same OEM part number, normalised the same way.
  4. Same short text, after normalising and applying the Equivalents sheet, so CONC and CONCENTRIC count as the same word.
  5. Similar specification: every key token, such as size, schedule and material family, appears in one master record.

Every match carries a HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW confidence level. Levels 1 to 3 are exact. Level 5 is a screen, not proof, so the template asks the requester to open the matched UIC and compare before answering.

A health check for your legacy master

Load your existing master and the Health Check sheet counts the likely duplicates already inside it: one UIC used for two items, the same part number on two codes, a part number listed as superseded on another item, identical descriptions, and the same words in a different order. Each group gets a parent row and child rows, so your team can review and merge codes one group at a time.

New requests and legacy cleansing are separate jobs. The template controls new items at the door; the health check shows you the size of the clean up behind it.

Missing attributes: found by AI, confirmed by a person

A spare cannot be bought correctly from a description that leaves out the size, the pressure class or the seat material. The Attribute Gaps sheet lists every item in your material master whose description is incomplete, names the attributes it is missing for its item type and gives a completeness figure. The attributes required for each item type are on the Attribute Rules sheet, where you can change them.

Where an item has a manufacturer part number, an AI assistant can look up the missing attributes on the manufacturer's website. The AI Lookup sheet holds the instructions and a ready made prompt for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot with web search switched on. No key and no macros are needed. What the AI finds goes onto the AI Findings sheet with the web address it came from.

The AI proposes, a person confirms. Every value stays UNVERIFIED until someone opens the source and confirms it, and only confirmed values count. A value the AI cannot find stays on the missing list. Nothing is guessed.

Line status: one word tells you where you stand

  • USE EXISTING UIC: the item already exists. No new request is needed.
  • RULE FAIL: a combination breaks a standard or a template rule. The message says which one.
  • INCOMPLETE: mandatory fields are empty, and the status counts them.
  • CHECK DUPLICATE: the master holds a matching or similar item. Use the matched UIC or explain why it is different.
  • READY TO SUBMIT: complete, rule clean and duplicate checked.

The Request Header rolls every line up into one request status, and the Approvals sheet records guarded sign offs.

Standards it follows

The dictionary structure follows the noun and modifier approach of ISO 22745 and ISO 8000, with PIDX used as a cross check for noun names. Equipment class follows ISO 14224. Line rules reference ASME B16 and B36 series, API 5L, ISO 3183, ASTM and EN pipe specifications, API 15 series for non metallic pipe, EN 10204 inspection certificates and the NACE sour service standards. Design code options cover ASME B31.3, B31.4, B31.8, ISO 13623 and EN 13480.

The References sheet lists every standard, what it governs in the workbook, the edition found when checked in October 2026, and the source. MESC is supported as an optional field for licence holders; the workbook contains no MESC codes.

Works with your ERP, not instead of it

The template is plain Excel with no macros, so it opens anywhere without enabling macros. It works alongside any ERP or EAM system from which you can export a material master. Your UIC stays the number your ERP assigns. Sheets are protected without a password, so your administrators can adapt the template to your own piping material specification.

About

I am Jitesh Mehta, a Supply Chain and EAM Transformation Executive based in Mumbai. I have held field level and senior client side roles at Shell, ADNOC Group (ZADCO), QatarEnergy, TotalEnergies, Petrofac and Crescent Petroleum, across Oil and Gas, Energy, EPC and offshore environments. I advise boards, CEOs, CSCOs and plant leaders on SAP S/4HANA, SAP Ariba, enterprise asset management, master data and procurement governance, always from the owner's side.

Independent. No vendor affiliation, no SI partnership.

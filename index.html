About

Jitesh Mehta is a Supply Chain and EAM Transformation Executive and the founder of JM Enterprise Transformation Advisory, based in Mumbai. He brings more than 35 years of field level and senior client side experience across Oil and Gas, Energy, EPC and offshore environments, including Shell, ADNOC Group (ZADCO), QatarEnergy, TotalEnergies, Petrofac and Crescent Petroleum. JM ETA advises boards, CEOs, CSCOs and plant leaders on SAP S/4HANA, SAP Ariba, enterprise asset management, master data and procurement governance, always from the owner's side.

Independent. No vendor affiliation, no SI partnership.
